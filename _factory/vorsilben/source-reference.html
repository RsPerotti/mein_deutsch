German Prefixes — The Feeling Map

Every German prefix carries a physical intuition before it carries a meaning. Learn the gesture, and hundreds of verbs stop being vocabulary and start being predictable.

Inseparable — glued to the verb, never moves, no ge- in the perfect (ich habe verkauft).  Separable — stressed, kicks to the end of the clause (ich rufe dich an).  Both — separable when literal/physical, inseparable when figurative.

Built as a feeling-first reference. The "why" column is a mnemonic bridge, not an etymology — a few are folk-logic rather than historical fact, but they hold the meaning in place.
